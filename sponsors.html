Independence policy

No provider ever pays to be suggested.

No hospital, clinic, IV/regen center, physician practice, or telehealth provider can pay Casanova for a listing, a badge, sponsored content, or a lead-gen fee — full stop. Institutions only appear here through Casanova's own free, earned verification. Sponsors never appear in Second Opinion reports, and the reviewing physician never sees who has sponsored anything.
