About Casanova Med

A focused service for people considering a consequential medical decision.

Patients pay Casanova Med directly for the service they purchase. Current prices are $299 for a written Second Opinion and $499 for a written opinion plus a call.

What the fee covers.

The Second Opinion fee covers case intake, secure record handling, physician review, and delivery of the selected written or written-plus-call service. Casanova Med is not the hospital or clinic providing any later treatment.

What is not for sale.

Casanova Med does not sell paid hospital or clinic placement. No provider can purchase inclusion in a Second Opinion result.

What we will disclose before purchase.

Any material physician-compensation structure or third-party commercial arrangement relevant to a service will be documented before we make a broader independence claim. Until that written confirmation is complete, the site does not claim that every relationship is commission-free.

Our limits.

Casanova Med is not emergency care and does not guarantee a recommendation, treatment, provider, destination, cost saving, or medical outcome.
