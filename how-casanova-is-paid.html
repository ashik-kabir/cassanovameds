Independence

Casanova Med is paid by patients, not by hospitals.

Here is exactly where our money comes from, and where it never comes from.

Second Opinion

You pay $299 or $499 directly, for a physician-reviewed opinion on whether pursuing care abroad is worth it at all.

Programs

If you later choose a Casanova program, you pay Casanova a coordination fee, itemized before you commit. That is why the review is separate: the physician's pay does not depend on whether you continue.

Providers

No hospital, clinic or other provider pays Casanova to be suggested, listed, featured or introduced to you, and none can buy placement.

Sponsors

Some non-provider brands, such as insurers and lodging, pay to be shown in the Brief or directory. They are always labeled "Sponsored" and never appear in Second Opinion reports.

This is the same reason our reviewing physician's pay does not depend on what the review recommends, and the same reason a hospital can never buy its way into a recommendation. If we ever accept a form of payment not described here, we will update this page before we take it.
